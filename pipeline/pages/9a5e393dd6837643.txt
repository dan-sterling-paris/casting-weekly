Peter Pan will be flying into The London Palladium this Christmas
Pantomimes
May 4
Written by Firewatch
We are delighted to announce that Peter Pan will be flying into The London Palladium this Christmas, marking Crossroads Pantomimes 8th incredible season at the theatre!
The West End’s ultimate pantomime adventure will star Jennifer Saunders as Captain Hook and Julian Clary as seaman Smee. The cast will also include Palladium panto favourites Paul Zerdin, Nigel Havers and Gary Wilmot, along with the return of Rob Madge following his debut in the West End's biggest panto last year.
Renowned as the most spectacular show staged in the West End with fabulous sets and costumes and jaw-dropping special effects, find out more at palladiumpantomime.com
