Blood Brothers announces 2027 tour stops
The show’s next spring tour has mapped out dates
Alex Wood
|
Tour
|
25 August 2026
The 2026 tour cast for Blood Brothers, photo by Jack Merriman
Tour stops has been confirmed for Blood Brothers for the first half of 2027.
Written by Willy Russell, Blood Brothers premiered as a play before being adapted into a musical. The story follows twins Mickey and Eddie, who are separated at birth and raised in different households before meeting again later in life.
The production is directed by Bob Tomson and Bill Kenwright, with resident director Tim Churchill. The creative team also includes music supervisor Matt Malone, set and costume designer Andy Walmsley, lighting designer Nick Richings and sound designer Dan Samson.
The spring 2027 tour will open at the Cliffs Pavilion in Southend (12 to 16 January), before visiting Southampton (19 to 23 January), Dartford (26 to 30 January), Sheffield (2 to 6 February), New Brighton (9 to 13 February), Cardiff (16 to 20 February), Eastbourne (23 to 27 February), Woking (2 to 6 March), Blackpool (9 to 13 March), Norwich (16 to 20 March), Billingham (23 to 27 March), Skegness (31 March to 3 April), Torquay (6 to 10 April), Manchester (13 to 24 April), Croydon (27 April to 1 May), Bath (4 to 8 May), Leeds (11 to 15 May) and Shrewsbury (18 to 22 May).
Casting for the spring tour will be announced at a later date, with 2026’s autumn tour cast now confirmed.
