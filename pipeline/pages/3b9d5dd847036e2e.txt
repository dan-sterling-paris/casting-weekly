Presented by Steph Connell Productions.
After his acting debut in  Snow White, Nizar discovers that panto is just for Christmas and there aren’t many theatre auditions when you’re four-foot-eight. How can he support his loved ones back home when the wolves are at the door?
Nizar joins fellow actor Alex on the night-time party circuit for a rollercoaster tour of the bright lights and dark corners of modern Britain.
Inspired by real events, The Entertainers  is a funny, tender and at times toe-curling journey into the hidden world of British entertainment. A road–trip movie of a play, it paints a portrait of the UK through the eyes of someone new to the country: the culture, the camaraderie, and the morning after.
From the team that brought you the award-winning tour de force Penguin, The Entertainers explores the line between empowerment and exploitation, as we follow Nizar on a quest for independence, joy, dignity and a bloody brilliant good night out.
By Hamzeh Al Hussien, Lindsay Rodden and Amy Golding
Co-commissioned by Liverpool Arab Arts Festival
Supported by Adopt a Playwright, Shubbak and Live Theatre.
