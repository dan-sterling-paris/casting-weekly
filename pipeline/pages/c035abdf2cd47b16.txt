Easter Pantomime: Rapunzel
Get ready… the magic is back!
KD Theatre Productions return with their hair-larious Easter panto tour of Rapunzel! Packed with laughter, music, dazzling costumes and colourful sets, it’s the perfect treat for the whole family this Easter.
When poor Rapunzel is trapped high in a tower by the Wicked Witch, will help arrive in time? Can the Queen and her friends save the day… or will Rapunzel be stuck letting her hair down forever?
Expect heaps of comedy, plenty of audience interaction, and all the laugh-out-loud moments you love!
From one of the UK’s award-winning pantomime producers, this is a joyful, high-energy show tangled with spectacle, fairy tale magic and unforgettable characters!
