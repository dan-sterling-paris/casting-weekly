New cast announced for MAMMA MIA! UK tour as hit musical continues through 2027
Fresh company joins the long-running ABBA musical ahead of new dates across the UK and Ireland
by
Staff Writer
June 22, 2026
Reading Time: 2 mins read
Jenn Griffin as Donna c Brinkhoff Moegenburg
The worldwide smash hit musical MAMMA MIA! has announced a new cast for its major UK tour, which continues to play across the country through to 2027.
Joining the tour are Lauren Woolf, Richard Taylor Woods, Simon Victor, Arvid Larsen, Hazel Baldwin, Samuel George Downey, Rachel Seirian, Lex Richardson, Ashton Sharp and Albert Green.
They are joined by Rubi-mai Bown, Georgia Gillam, Bethany Lythgoe, Sullivan Martin, Adam Stickler and Luke Stone, while Jenn Griffin, Ashleigh Jones and Sarah Earnshaw continue in their roles alongside the current company.
The new cast will begin performances from 13 August 2026 at King’s Theatre Glasgow, before visiting Belfast, Sheffield, Nottingham, Milton Keynes, Wolverhampton, Bradford, Cardiff, Liverpool and Plymouth.
Created and produced by Judy Craymer, MAMMA MIA! combines the timeless songs of ABBA with a story of a mother, a daughter and three possible fathers set on a Greek island.

Judy Craymer said “I’m excited to welcome some new cast members into the MAMMA MIA! UK Tour family and can’t wait for them to join us on the journey, bringing their talent to these roles through until 2027. Our much-loved musical tells the story of a mother, a daughter and three possible dads, inspired by ABBA’s irresistible hits, and continues to delight audiences of all generations as they escape to our Greek island idyll.”
Now in its 28th year, the musical has been seen by more than 70 million people worldwide and remains one of the most successful shows in theatrical history.
Featuring music and lyrics by Benny Andersson and Björn Ulvaeus, a book by Catherine Johnson, direction by Phyllida Lloyd and choreography by Anthony Van Laast, the production continues to captivate audiences around the globe.
