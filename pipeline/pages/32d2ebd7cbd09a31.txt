Full cast announced for Jersey Boys 20th anniversary UK and Ireland tour
Smash-hit musical returns with major new tour opening in Wimbledon in June 2026
by
Staff Writer
May 11, 2026
Reading Time: 2 mins read
LtoR Carlo Boumouglbay as Tommy DeVito, Luke Baker as Frankie Valli, Toby Miles as Bob Guadio & Lewis Kennedy as Nick Massi, credit Matt Crockett
The full cast has been announced for the 20th anniversary UK and Ireland tour of Jersey Boys, which opens at New Wimbledon Theatre on 17 June 2026.
The Tony and Olivier Award-winning musical will tour extensively through 2027, with further dates still to be announced.
Luke Baker will lead the cast as Frankie Valli, alongside Carlo Boumouglbay as Tommy DeVito, Lewis Kennedy as Nick Massi, Toby Miles as Bob Gaudio and Ellis Kirk as Alternate Frankie Valli.
Joining them on tour are Aiden Carson, Artemis Chrisoulakis, Sydnie Hocknell, Michael Levi, Olivia Mitchell, Jarryd Nurden, Oliver Tester, Damien Winchester and Fed Zanni.
The company is completed by swings Scott Goncalves, Melissa Potts, Zach Sorrow and Joshua Spencer-Pepper.

Jersey Boys features a book by Marshall Brickman and Rick Elice, with music by Bob Gaudio and lyrics by Bob Crewe, and charts the rise of Frankie Valli and The Four Seasons from New Jersey street corners to international superstardom.
The musical includes hit songs such as Sherry, Big Girls Don’t Cry, Walk Like A Man, Can’t Take My Eyes Off You and Oh What a Night.
The 20th anniversary tour is staged by the original Broadway creative team, led by director Des McAnuff and choreographer Sergio Trujillo, with musical supervision by Ron Melrose.
Jersey Boys has won 65 major awards worldwide and has been seen by more than 30 million people, enjoying record-breaking runs on Broadway, in the West End and on tour.
