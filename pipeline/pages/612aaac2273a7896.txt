Join us on a wild ride to a magical far-off land, for the astounding adventure of Aladdin! Can the plucky Aladdin prove himself to The Princess? And will the wicked sorcerer get his hands on the legendary lamp? With the help of a hilarious genie and some panto magic, anything is possible!
With Nottingham’s favourite Dame John Elkington leading our fantastic cast, Aladdin promises to be a rip-roaring adventure bursting with spectacular costumes, amazing special effects, and comedy chaos at every turn. It’s a treasure trove of family fun in this festive spectacular that will make all your wishes come true!

Access
We provide British Sign Language Interpretation, Captioning, and Audio Description for this production, as well as Dementia Friendly, Reduced Capacity, and Relaxed performances.Everyone is welcome to attend accessible performances. Find out more about Access at Nottingham Playhouse here.
Schools and Groups
Groups
Groups of 10 or more can save up to 20% on full price tickets.
Discount will be automatically applied on the ‘Checkout’ screen. Book up to 20 tickets online with the groups discount, discounts vary per performance and are subject to availability.
See the list of discounted performances on the Group Discounts Page.
Have a large group looking to attend? Please email tickets@nottinghamplayhouse.co.uk and a member of the team will be able to assist.
Schools
Show tickets for school pupils are £15.00 each (one teacher goes free with every 10 tickets purchased).
Find out more about School Visits here and book by calling Box Office on 0115 941 9419.
Schools early booking offer: Pay in full by start of Easter Holidays (Friday 27 March 2026) and pay £13.50 per ticket. Pay by end of School Year (Monday 27 July 2026) and pay £14.50 per ticket.
Pay in 3
Our Panto Payment Plan
Our Pay in 3 scheme allows you to split the cost of your booking for our 2026/27 panto Aladdin in to three payments. Your first payment is made when you book your tickets and is followed by two monthly payments. Find out more.
