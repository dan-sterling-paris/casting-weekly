AIDA CRUISES - PARIS AUDITIONS
We are thrilled to announce our first ever auditions for AIDA Cruises in Paris, France. Auditions for dancers will take place in Central Paris on Monday, 28 November 2023.
Aida Cruises, the German subsidiary of the Carnival Group, is the leading player in the German cruise market covering destinations from South America to Europe and from Asia to the Caribbean, with 14 ships in 2023. The contracts begin with a rehearsal period of between 5 and 7 weeks in Hamburg, followed by up to 6 month contract on any of their ships including AIDAbella, AIDAviva, AIDAblu, AIDAstella, AIDAaura, AIDAvita, AIDAcara.
We positively encourage applications from male/female/non-binary performers of all ethnicities and heritages.
APPLICANTS MUST BE OVER 18 YEARS OF AGE.
