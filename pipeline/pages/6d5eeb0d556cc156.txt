Full Cast Announced for UK and Ireland Tour of Punch
Bailey West leads James Graham’s Olivier Award-winning play as the production embarks on a major tour from Nottingham Playhouse
by
Staff Writer
November 19, 2026
Reading Time: 2 mins read
Cast of Punch on Tour Image supplied by publicist without credit
The full cast has been announced for the UK and Ireland tour of James Graham’s multi award-winning play Punch, which opens at Nottingham Playhouse in January 2027.
Directed by Nottingham Playhouse Artistic Director Adam Penford and based on Jacob Dunne’s memoir Right from Wrong, the production will tour the UK and Ireland from 25 January to 24 April 2027.
Making his stage debut in the role of Jacob is Nottingham actor Bailey West, whose screen credits include Sky’s Charmer.
West is joined by Gail Kemp, Sally Ann Matthews, Mark Meadows, Mica Ricketts and Elan Butler, who reprises his acclaimed performance as Raf/Sam. The company also includes understudies Grace Elken and Laurence James-Davis.
Punch tells the true story of Jacob, a teenager from Nottingham whose life is changed forever after a single impulsive act of violence leads to devastating consequences.

Following a prison sentence and a search for meaning, Jacob is confronted by an extraordinary act of compassion when the parents of his victim, Joan and David, agree to meet him, setting him on a path towards redemption and transformation.
The production has become one of the most celebrated new British plays of recent years, winning Best New Play at the Olivier Awards, Critics’ Circle Awards and The Standard Awards.
The West End production also received the Theatre Prize at the Sky Arts Awards 2026 and became the first new play to open simultaneously in the West End and on Broadway.
The creative team includes production designer Anna Fleischle, lighting designer Robbie Butler, sound designer and composer Alexandra Faye Braithwaite, movement director Leanne Pinder and casting director Christopher Worrall CDG.
The tour will visit Nottingham Playhouse, Theatre Royal Bath, Birmingham Rep, Chichester Festival Theatre, Malvern Theatres, Blackpool Grand Theatre, Curve Leicester, BEAM Hertford, Glasgow CITZ, Dublin’s Gaiety Theatre, Cambridge Arts Theatre and Coventry’s Belgrade Theatre.
The production is dedicated to James Hodgkinson and all victims of one-punch violence.
