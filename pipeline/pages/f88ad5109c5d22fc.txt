Now in its record breaking 11th year, THRILLER LIVE is a spectacular concert created to celebrate the career of the world’s greatest entertainer, with dazzling choreography by the show’s award-winning director, Gary Lloyd.
After opening in the Lyric Theatre in London on 2 January 2009 to rave reviews, Thriller Live has celebrated its ninth birthday in the West End, embarked on a world tour and a sell-out UK arena tour, played over 5000 shows globally to over 4 million fans, visited over 30 countries and entered the West End record books by becoming the longest running show in the Lyric Theatre’s 125 year history and is about to overtake Miss Saigon as the 13th longest running musical in the West End. It is currently booking until April 2020.
We are looking for
MALE AND FEMALE SINGERS
to play and cover roles
All will need excellent pop/rock voices with VERY HIGH ranges – exciting, charismatic and energetic performers - some with a more soul sound, some with a dramatic rock sound and loud belt. Must have good movement skills.
Must be black/ African Caribbean /dual heritage.
Playing ages approximately 18 - 30.
MALE ROCK SINGER
Will need excellent pop/rock voices with VERY HIGH ranges – exciting, charismatic and energetic performers with a dramatic rock sound and loud belt.
Must have good movement skills.
Performers of all ethnicities/heritages considered
Playing age approximately 18 - 30.
