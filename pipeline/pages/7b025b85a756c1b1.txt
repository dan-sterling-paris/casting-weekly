About Lyceum Youth Theatre Juniors (11-13s) Spring Term 2027
Weekly Sessions and Term Dates:
- Mondays 5-7pm, 11th Jan- 22nd March
- Saturday 1-3pm, 16th Jan - 27th March
- Saturday 3-5pm, 16th Jan - 27th March
· Cost per Term: £100
During the term, participants will begin to further explore theatrical conventions and practitioners, deepening their understanding and creating the foundations needed to begin creating their own work. We look to engage in play, improvisation and other skills, to help participants creatively express their own ideas.
