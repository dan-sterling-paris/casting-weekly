About A Christmas Carol
Chill winds sweep through the gas-lit streets of Victorian Edinburgh as Dickens’ beloved classic comes to life at The Lyceum for our 2026 festive season.
On Christmas Eve, three mysterious spirits whisk Ebenezer Scrooge away on a whirlwind journey through his past, present, and future. Will he discover the true meaning of Christmas before it’s too late?
Directed by our Award-winning Artistic Director, James Brining, let this festive classic fill you with cheer - join us for a Christmas tradition in the making.
