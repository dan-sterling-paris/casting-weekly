THRILLER LIVE is a spectacular concert created to celebrate the career of the world’s greatest entertainer with dazzling choreography by the show’s award-winning director, Gary Lloyd. THRILLER LIVE marks the first Jackson-dedicated live theatrical experience ever.
We hold auditions throughout the year for
YOUNG MALE PERFORMERS
to sing the songs of the young Michael Jackson
Young Male, aged approximately 9-14
He will need an excellent unbroken pop/rock voice with a high range – exciting, confident and energetic performer. Songs include I Want You Back, ABC and I’ll Be There.
Black/African Caribbean /dual heritage
Schedule 9, Part 1 of the Equality Act 2010; (Occupational Requirements) applies
Venues : Lyric Theatre, West End and international touring
For purely practical reasons, we are not able to consider children who live more than approximately one hour from central London.
