Now in its record breaking 10th year, THRILLER LIVE is a spectacular concert created to celebrate the career of the world’s greatest entertainer, with dazzling choreography by the show’s award-winning director, Gary Lloyd.
After opening in the Lyric Theatre in London on 2 January 2009 to rave reviews, Thriller Live has celebrated its ninth birthday in the West End, embarked on a world tour and a sell-out UK arena tour, played over 5000 shows globally to over 4 million fans, visited over 30 countries and entered the West End record books by becoming the longest running show in the Lyric Theatre’s 125 year history and the 16th longest running musical in the West End.
We are are always looking for singers for the West End and touring companies throughout the year – dates and contract lengths vary.
MALE AND FEMALE SINGERS
to play and cover roles
All will need excellent pop/rock voices with VERY HIGH ranges – exciting, charismatic and energetic performers - some with a more soul sound, some with a dramatic rock sound and loud belt. Must have good movement skills.
Must be black/ African Caribbean /dual heritage.
Playing ages approximately 18 - 30.
MALE ROCK SINGER
Will need excellent pop/rock voices with VERY HIGH ranges – exciting, charismatic and energetic performers with a dramatic rock sound and loud belt.
Must have good movement skills.
Performers of all ethnicities/heritages welcome to apply
Playing age approximately 18 - 30.
YOUNG MALE PERFORMERS
to sing the songs of the young Michael Jackson
Young Male, aged approximately 9-14
He will need an excellent unbroken pop/rock voice with a high range – exciting, confident and energetic performer. Songs include I Want You Back, ABC and I’ll Be There.
Must be black/African Caribbean /dual heritage
Schedule 9, Part 1 of the Equality Act 2010; (Occupational Requirements) applies
