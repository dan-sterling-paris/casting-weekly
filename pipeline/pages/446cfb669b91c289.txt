The Silence of the Lambs makes thrilling world stage premiere in Leicester
UK
Aug 12
Written by Firewatch
John Partridge (Hannibal Lecter) - Photography by Marc Brenner
We came face to face with Dr Hannibal Lecter and Buffalo Bill and survived at the world stage premiere of the iconic psychological thriller in Leicester.
L to R Producer James Lane, director Nikolai Foster, writer Gina Gionfriddo and producer Ed McGovern.
The company of The Silence of the Lambs with Nikolai Foster and Gina Gionfriddo. Photos by Beth Walsh
Written by the award-winning Gina Gionfriddo and directed by the acclaimed Nikolai Foster, The Silence of the Lambs is now open and chilling audiences at Curve, before it heads off on its UK and Ireland tour.
This electrifying new production stars John Partridge (EastEnders, Cabaret) as Hannibal Lecter, Mollie Gallagher (Coronation Street) as Clarice Starling, Oliver Farnworth (Emmerdale) as Agent Jack Crawford, and Sam Jackson (Skins) as Buffalo Bill and is based on Thomas Harris's gripping literary masterpiece.
