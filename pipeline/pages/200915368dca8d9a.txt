Wishing Magic Mike London a happy 4th birthday!
Nov 10
Written by Jan Baister
Cast of Magic Mike Live, London. Photo: Matt Crockett
Today we’re celebrating 4 unforgettable years of Magic Mike Live at the Hippodrome Casino, London!
Conceived and directed by Channing Tatum, the London production has been seen by an incredible 400,000 people and features some of the world’s sexiest, most talented guys performing sizzling dance routines, in an unforgettably fun piece of 360-degree entertainment, based on the hit movies.
Happy birthday guys!
For tickets and information, visit MagicMikeLondon.co.uk
