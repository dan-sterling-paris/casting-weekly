Why see Sinatra The Musical?
Start Spreadin' The News
Sinatra The Musical is swaggering into the West End's Aldwych Theatre, following a highly successful 2023 debut in Birmingham! Backed by a world-class creative team, including a book by two-time Tony winner Joe DiPietro (Memphis, What's New Pussycat?) and directed by Olivier and three-time Tony winner Kathleen Marshall (Anything Goes, Top Hat).
From career freefall to the greatest comeback in showbiz, this is the story of a man who lived it his way, packed with over 20 of his timeless hits, such as One for My Baby, That's Life, and The Best Is Yet to Come!
Come Fly With Sinatra!
New Year's Eve, 1942. At the Paramount Theatre in New York, a skinny 27-year-old Hoboken kid is set to take the stage. One voice, one night - and suddenly the whole world is listening. As Frank Sinatra sings his way into history, his private life hits a sour note: torn between his wife Nancy and movie goddess Ava Gardner, with the press circling and scandal threatening to knock him flat.
Affectionately known as "Ol' Blue Eyes" by fans of all generations, Frank Sinatra enjoyed a career that spanned five decades. He sold more than 150 million records worldwide, won multiple Grammy Awards and an Academy Award, becoming the gold standard for jazz, swing, and pop.
