The Olivier Awards nominations are announced
UK
Mar 5
Written by Firewatch
With Spring finally in the air in London it was time for the annual Olivier Award nominations to be announced.
Imelda Staunton and the company of Hello, Dolly!
We are delighted that there are nods for Mean Girls (co-produced with Sonia Friedman and Lorne Michaels) for Tom Xander for Best Supporting Actor in a Musical and Hugh Durrant for Best Costume Design for Robin Hood (Crossroads Pantomimes).
It’s wonderful to see Imelda Staunton nominated for her 14th Olivier Award! This time she is up for Best Actress in a Musical for Hello, Dolly! (co-produced with Michael Harrison), which is also nominated for Best Musical Revival, with Andy Nyman nominated for Best Actor in a Supporting Role in a Musical.
Julian Clary in a Hugh Durrant Olivier-nominated costume for Robin Hood
Tom Xander in Mean Girls
Huge congratulations to Crossroads Pantomimes CEO Michael Harrison and the team at Michael Harrison Entertainment and Lloyd Webber Harrison Musicals, who are nominated for an incredible ten Awards across four productions, Hello, Dolly, Robin Hood, Titanique and Starlight Express.
We can’t wait to celebrate the talent and the magic of theatre at the Royal Albert Hall on 6 April!
Full list of nominations can be seen here.
