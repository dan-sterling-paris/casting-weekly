Jason Donovan stars in the 50th Anniversary Tour of The Rocky Horror Show in Sydney 2023
Oct 25
Written by Jan Baister
Jason Donovan will star in The Rocky Horror Show in 2023. Photo: Hugo Glendinning
Last week we were shivering in antici-pation, before the on sale of our brand-new Australian production of the iconic Rock n’ Roll musical, which has been seen by over 30 million people in over 30 countries!
The Rocky Horror Show will open in February 2023 at Theatre Royal Sydney to celebrate 50 legendary years. For more info and tickets visit rockyhorror.com.au
