Exclusive: Jersey Boys announces anniversary tour cast
A cast for all seasons!
Alex Wood
|
Tour
|
11 May 2026
Carlo Boumouglbay as Tommy DeVito, Luke Baker as Frankie Valli, Toby Miles as Bob Guadio and Lewis Kennedy as Nick Massi, photo by Matt Crockett
Exclusive: Casting has been revealed for the new UK and Ireland tour of Jersey Boys.
The production opens at New Wimbledon Theatre on 17 June 2026 as part of the show’s 20th anniversary celebrations, with dates currently booking throughout 2027. You can see the current set of tour dates at the link below.
The musical follows the story of Frankie Valli and The Four Seasons, and features songs including “Sherry”, “Walk Like A Man”, “Big Girls Don’t Cry”, “Beggin’” and “Can’t Take My Eyes Off You”. The piece has a book by Marshall Brickman and Rick Elice, music by Bob Gaudio and lyrics by Bob Crewe.
Luke Baker will play Frankie Valli, with Carlo Boumouglbay as Tommy DeVito, Lewis Kennedy as Nick Massi and Toby Miles as Bob Gaudio. Ellis Kirk will be the alternate Frankie Valli.
The cast also includes Aiden Carson as Joey, Artemis Chrisoulakis as Francine, Sydnie Hocknell as Mary Delgado, Michael Levi as Bob Crewe, Olivia Mitchell as Lorraine, Jarryd Nurden as Hank Majewski, Oliver Tester as Norm Waxman, Damien Winchester as Barry Belson and Fed Zanni as Gyp DeCarlo. Swings are Scott Goncalves, Melissa Potts, Zach Sorrow and Joshua Spencer-Pepper.
Jersey Boys first opened on Broadway in 2005 before transferring to the West End in 2008, where it ran for nine years. The production later returned to London at Trafalgar Theatre from 2021 to 2024. Previous UK and Ireland tours ran between 2014 and 2022.
The tour reunites the original Broadway creative team, with direction by Des McAnuff and choreography by Sergio Trujillo. Musical supervision, vocal arrangements and incidental music are by Ron Melrose, with associate direction and choreography by Danny Austin, scenic design by Klara Zieglerova, costume design by Jess Goldstein, lighting design by Howell Binkley, sound design by Steve Canyon Kennedy and projection design by Michael Clark.
Orchestrations are by Steve Orich, casting is by Jill Green, associate UK musical supervision is by Ed Bussey, musical direction is by Lauren Ronan and resident direction is by Luke Zammit. General management is by Dodgers UK Productions.
