Let your imagination take flight this half-term.
Join us for the world premiere of a brand-new production of The Snowman™ as it comes back home to Birmingham and The Rep.
For 32 years, The Snowman has enchanted theatre audiences, now, discover the next chapter of this much-loved story. Based on Raymond Briggs’ cherished book, The Rep’s sparkling new production features Howard Blake’s iconic score including the classic Walking in the Air, beautifully crafted puppets and of course, a gravity-defying friendship.
“I want adults to become children and children never to forget it.”
Will Tuckett (Director)
When a young boy’s snowman magically comes to life an unforgettable adventure begins. Together, they journey through the night, gliding hand-in-hand across the starlit sky. Along the way they encounter Father Christmas, friendly reindeer, and a joyful gathering of snow people beneath the shimmering Northern Lights, discovering a world of wonder, friendship and winter magic.
Directed and choreographed by Olivier Award-winning Will Tuckett (West Side Story (RSC/The Sage Gateshead), Into the Woods (Royal Opera House), and The Nutcracker (National Ballet of Japan)), production design by the multi award-winning Anna Fleischle (Punch, 2:22 A Ghost Story, Everybody’s Talking About Jamie and Hangmen). With award-winning designer Tahra Zafar, best known for her recreation of Paddington Bear for the West End – bringing our iconic snowman to life.
Come and make a new snowman memory.
Rediscover the joy of the story you love. Share the wonder of the magic you remember. And experience The Snowman with family and friends, as you’ve never seen it before.

Aaron Smyth
Aaron Smyth
At just 17 years old, Aaron Smyth left home in Australia to pursue his dream of becoming a professional ballet dancer. Since then, he has been a company member with the American Ballet Theatre Studio Company in New York, The Royal Ballet in London – where he created a role in Titian Metamorphosis, a collaboration with the National Gallery – and The Joffrey Ballet in Chicago. He was also an original cast member of London’s West End production of An American in Paris, choreographed and directed by Christopher Wheeldon.
Aaron has performed for royalty, the Obamas, and audiences around the world as he continues to travel internationally as a Principal Guest Artist. He has appeared on two editions of the Got Talent franchise, placing third on Australia’s Got Talent and reaching the quarter-finals of America’s Got Talent. He is also featured in the Netflix ballet documentary First Position.
A Gold Medal and Audience Choice Award winner at the prestigious Genée International Ballet Competition, Aaron has also earned five additional medals at international ballet competitions. His screen credits include Disney’s The Nutcracker and the Four Realms, where he portrayed the Snow Cavalier alongside the acclaimed ballerina Misty Copeland.
Most recently, Aaron performed the role of Older Billy in the 10th Anniversary Australian tour of Billy Elliot the Musical. Alongside his performing career, he teaches and guest instructs internationally throughout Singapore, the United States, and Europe. He serves on the Advisory Board for the Ballet Championships of America and is a guest instructor for Dancerpalooza.
Most recently, Aaron performed as a Principal Guest Artist in Tesseract: The Geometry of Truth in Mumbai, India, a production presented by the Times of India and directed by renowned choreographer Shiamak Davar.

Director & Choreographer
Will Tuckett
Will Tuckett
Will Tuckett is an award-winning Director and Choreographer. He danced as a member, then guest artist of The Royal Ballet for 30 years and has created more than 25 shows for the Royal Opera House, including the Olivier Award-winning The Wind in the Willows (the first Royal Opera House show to ever have a West End transfer), Elizabeth (RBO / Barbican theatre) , ‘Diana and Actaeon’ (Metamorphosis: Titian 2012), The Seven Deadly Sins, The Soldier’s Tale, Pinocchio, Faeries, Timecode, The Thief of Baghdad and Pleasure’s Progress all for The Royal Ballet and The Royal Opera House.
His recent choreographic work includes a new production of The Nutcracker and a Macbeth for the National Ballet of Japan and a revival of his Pinocchio for The National Ballet of Canada. The Death of Gesualdo for Concert Theatre Works (London / New York), Love Life for Opera North, Mirrored for BRB2.
His 2022 production Then or Now for Ballet Black was nominated for the 2022 Southbank Award and UK Theatre Awards Achievement in Dance and his Changing Light for Sarasota Ballet was this year revived for fifth time.
He has worked as Movement Director for various theatre, musical theatre and opera productions including work for The Royal Opera, Opera North, Welsh National Opera, The Bregenz Festival, The St. Poelten Festival, Grange Park Opera, The RSC, Shakespeare’s Globe, The Almeida Theatre, The Soho Theatre, Theatre Clwyd, The Stephen Joseph Theatre.
He has worked on several feature films as choreographer including the recent 87North action movie Pretty Lethal, the animated feature film Scrooge for Netflix, Streetdance 3D for Vertigo films and Muse with the Oscar nominated actor Geza Rohrig. He was co-director and choreographer with The Quay Brothers on The Sandman for Channel 4 and Fur Alina for the BBC, and as choreographer on the multi award winning NELA directed by Andy Margetson.
Several of his stage works have been filmed for television in the UK, U.S.A. and Japan.
He has directed theatre, musical theatre and opera including the first new full staging of West Side Story in the UK (Royal Shakespeare Company / Sage Gateshead), Sondheim’s Into the Woods (Royal Opera House), Humperdinck’s Hansel and Gretel (Iford Opera) and Italiana in Algeri (Garsington Opera), the new play Lady Macbeth (Uri Otemachi Hall, Tokyo) Peter Handke’s KASPAR (Tokyo Metropolitan Theatre), Tom Stoppard’s Every Good Boy Deserves Favour and Peter Shaffer’s Royal Hunt of The Sun (Parco Theatre, Tokyo) , The Crane Maiden (Kanegawa Arts Theatre, Yokohama), Moira Buffini’s Marianne Dreams (Almeida Theatre, London).
He has been commissioned by Whitechapel Gallery, Tate Modern and The National Gallery to create and develop work in response to, and as part of, various exhibitions.
He was the first ROH2 Creative Associate for The Royal Opera House (2006) and has just been made Creative Director of a new UK wide National Schools Digital Campaign by the RBO Schools department, focussing on Narrative and Dance for children aged 7-11 .
He was the Clore Leadership Dance Fellow where he chose a secondment with Climate Emergency charity Tipping Point (2008) and became an accredited Relational Dynamics coach (2010).
He is a guest lecturer for the Royal College of Fashion, Wimbledon College of Art and Nottingham Trent University. He also works as a freelance arts consultant in the UK and internationally and is a governor of the Royal Ballet Companies and School.
