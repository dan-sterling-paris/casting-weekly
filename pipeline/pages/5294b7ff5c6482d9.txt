Tom Chambers joins Giovanna Fletcher in Before I Go To Sleep tour
It kicks off in January
Tanyel Gumushan
|
Tour
|
21 October 2026
Tom Chambers, headshot provided uncredited
The stage adaptation of Before I Go To Sleep has added further cast.
S J Watson’s novel follows Christine, who appears to live an idyllic life, except that every night when she falls asleep, she loses all memory of the last 20 years. As she attempts to piece her life back together, terrifying truths emerge. It was adapted into a hit film starring Nicole Kidman and Colin Firth.
As already revealed, Giovanna Fletcher will lead the world premiere stage show, written by Rachel Wagstaff and directed by Loveday Ingram. They previously collaborated on The Girl on the Train.
Joining her, Tom Chambers will play Ben on the UK and Ireland tour. He said: “I’m absolutely thrilled to be joining Giovanna in Before I Go To Sleep. It’s such a brilliantly unsettling story, and what I love about it is that you’re never quite sure who or what to believe.”
The actor added: “Ben is a fascinating character to play, particularly in a story where memory and trust are so closely intertwined. I think Rachel has done a great job adapting the book into a stage play and I can’t wait to get into rehearsals and bring this extraordinary thriller to audiences around the country.”
A sequel to the novel will also be released in 2027.
Before I Go To Sleep will open at Richmond Theatre from 15 to 23 January 2027. It will then visit Bath Theatre Royal (26 to 30 January), Chichester Festival Theatre (2 to 6 February), Truro Hall for Cornwall (9 to 13 February), Woking New Victoria Theatre (16 to 20 February), Cardiff New Theatre (23 to 27 February), York Grand Opera House (2 to 6 March), Milton Keynes Theatre (9 to 13 March), Cheltenham Everyman Theatre (16 to 20 March), Norwich Theatre Royal (23 to 27 Marc), Sheffield Lyceum (30 March to 3 April), Birmingham Alexandra Theatre (13 to 17 April), Chester Storyhouse (20 to 24 April), Dublin Gaiety Theatre (27 April to 1 May), Lowry in Salford (4 to 8 May), Shrewsbury Theatre Severn (11 to 15 May), Newcastle Theatre Royal (18 to 22 May), Edinburgh King’s Theatre (25 to 29 May), Glasgow Theatre Royal (1 to 5 June), Nottingham Theatre Royal (15 to 19 June), and Leeds Grand (22 to 26 June).
Chambers is scheduled to appear at venues up to and including Sheffield Lyceum.
The show has set, costume and video design by Justin Nardella, lighting design by Jason Taylor, sound design by Gareth Fry and is produced by Josh Andrews. Further casting and creative team announcements will follow.
