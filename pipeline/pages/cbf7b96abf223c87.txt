It’s all about connection at USITT 2024 in Seattle
Apr 10
Written by Firewatch
The Production and Humanity teams from Crossroads Live North America exhibited at this year’s United States Institute for Theatre Technology Conference (USITT), which took place in Seattle.
USITT offers an incredible opportunity for networking with technical vendors, schools, and other theatrical companies, with many panels on different aspects of the industry, ranging from educational, regional and commercial theatre.
The team had a booth, which showcased the upcoming season, and connected with hundreds of potential candidates about open crew and management positions.
They left the Emerald City energized and excited to attend again next year in Columbus, Ohio.
