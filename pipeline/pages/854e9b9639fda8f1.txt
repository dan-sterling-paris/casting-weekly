PRISCILLA THE PARTY!
SUPERNUMERARIES/AUDIENCE MANAGERS
Any gender
Any age
Any height
Any appearance
Contract info: pay is £60 per performance/rehearsal (minimum 3 hour call). It is an on-your-feet / active role.
PRISCILLA: THE PARTY! is looking for a pool of Supernumeraries/Audience Managers for its upcoming launch in March 2024 at OUTERNET, directly next to Tottenham Court Road station.
The production is immersive and the roles involve some improvisation. This role requires interacting with audiences during the performance and directing them during some scene changes.
Supernumeraries/Audience Managers must be available for at least 4 performances per week, but availability for all 7-8 is ideal. Sunday shows are frequent, and there will be up to two shows per day.
Previews begin on the 1st of March 2024 and the run is open ended. Supernumeraries/Audience Mangers will be required for some rehearsal at the venue at the end of February (dates TBC).
We are looking for Supernumeraries/Managers for 4-8 performances per week.
Supernumeraries/Audience Managers with experience in improvisation, ASM work, or side-hustle as ushers are especially welcome!
Priscilla: The Musical dazzled audiences when it opened in 2006 and continues to tour the globe. Now, PRISCILLA: THE PARTY! transports audiences to the Australian outback to enjoy a dazzling array of eye-popping costumes, and a night of dancing along to a gyrating hit parade of dance floor classics.
