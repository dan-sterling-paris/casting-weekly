Back To The Future: The Musical is now open and is delighting audiences at the Sydney Lyric theatre with an electrifying Australian cast led by Tony Award-winner Roger Bart as ‘Doc Brown’ and sensational newcomer Axel Duffy at the wheel as Marty McFly.
Sydney’s Ashleigh Rubenach plays Marty’s mum Lorraine Baines, Ethan Jones plays George McFly, Javon King plays the roles of Goldie Wilson and Marvin Berry, and Thomas McGuane plays the pivotal role of Biff in this theatrical experience packed with heart, humour and high-octane spectacle.
Time travel never looked so good - book your tickets yesterday!
