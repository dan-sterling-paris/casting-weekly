Singin’ in the Rain to play Christmas season at Sheffield Theatres
Smash-hit Chichester Festival Theatre production heads to the Crucible ahead of major UK tour
by
Staff Writer
May 14, 2026
Reading Time: 4 mins read
Singin' in the Rain Image supplied by publicist
The Chichester Festival Theatre and Stage Entertainment production of Singin’ in the Rain will play a Christmas season at Sheffield Theatres’ Crucible from 26 November 2026 to 16 January 2027.
Presented by Sheffield Theatres in association with Jonathan Church Theatre Productions, the much-loved musical returns to the venue where director Jonathan Church began his career.
Based on the classic Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer film, the production brings the glamour of 1920s Hollywood to the stage with high-energy choreography, lavish designs and more than 14,000 litres of water used on stage.
The story follows silent film stars Don Lockwood and Lina Lamont as the arrival of talking pictures throws their careers into chaos, forcing them to adapt with the help of an aspiring young actress.
Featuring the iconic score by Nacio Herb Brown and Arthur Freed, the show includes classics such as “Good Morning”, “Make ‘em Laugh” and the title song “Singin’ in the Rain”.
Following its Sheffield run, the production will embark on a major UK tour, visiting venues in Southend, Belfast, Dublin, Sunderland, Canterbury, Liverpool, Glasgow, Edinburgh, Birmingham and beyond through to September 2027.

The production is directed by Jonathan Church, with choreography by Andrew Wright and additional choreography by Jayne Elster.
Design is by Simon Higlett, with lighting design by Tim Mitchell, sound design by Gareth Owen and video design by Ian Galloway.
