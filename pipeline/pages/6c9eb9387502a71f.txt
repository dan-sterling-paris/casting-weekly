Biography
Originally from Los Angeles and now based in Prague, Maya Kvetny is a distinguished casting director with a successful track record in feature films and TV series targeting the international market. Maya’s impressive portfolio includes casting for high-profile productions such as Marvel’s “Spiderman: Far From Home,” Amazon’s “Carnival Row,” the “Das Boot” series, the Oscar-winning film “Jojo Rabbit,” Netflix’s “Extraction 2” and “The Gray Man,” as well as Ron Howard’s series, “Genius.”
Throughout her career, a unique expression has been the common thread that defines Maya’s work. Her keen eye for talent and her ability to nurture the creation of authentic, memorable characters have made her a sought-after professional in the industry. Recently, Maya has taken on the role of mentoring actors, offering coaching from the unique perspective of a casting director with extensive experience collaborating with some of the most prominent directors in the field.
Maya Kvetny is passionate about supporting actors in their journey, helping them to bring their most authentic selves to their roles and create characters that leave a lasting impact on audiences.
