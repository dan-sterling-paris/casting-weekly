Birmingham Rep announces full casting for Peter Pan
Posted on Thu 10 Sep
Share
Birmingham Rep has announced full casting for its forthcoming festive production of Peter Pan, J M Barrie’s much-loved children’s story, which runs between Wednesday 18 November 2026 and Sunday 17 January 2027.
This new production of Peter Pan is adapted by Phil Porter, with original music by Ruth Chan, and is directed by The Rep’s Artistic Director, Joe Murphy, fresh from his success with Sweeney Todd The Demon Barber of Fleet Street.
The cast comprises: Jack Benjamin (Mr Darling/Skylights); Amber Sylvia Edwards (Wendy); Maddie Hansen (Curly); Matthew James Hinchliffe (Jukes); James Ifan (Tootles); Jordan Laviniere (Michael Darling); Jacob Leeson (Slightly); Lara Lewis (Nibs); Hannah McPake (Hook/Mrs Darling); Elijah Millar (John Darling); Emmy Stonelake (Tinkerbell); Susannah Van Den Berg (Smee); and Iverson Yabut (Peter Pan).
Peter Pan is the classic story of the boy who never grew up and his adventures in Neverland with Wendy and the other Darling children, not forgetting Tinker Bell, The Lost Boys and Hook. Bursting with imagination, this faithful re-telling of J M Barrie’s beloved tale looks set to delight audiences afresh this festive season.
To book, visit: birmingham-rep.co.uk
