TALK
We firmly believe that in the performing arts industry that, too often, we encounter performers who are having a tough time. We want to do something about this.
We are committed to offering a place, once-a-month, for any performer to come and TALK to other performers and creatives. About anything.
There's no cost, no obligation, just turn up and talk to other people.
What do we want out of this? Nothing. We merely want performers to feel like they can talk about the industry, openly and safely, without prejudice. That's it. Simple.
​Plus, we'll give you a glass of wine.
You can register your interest via the following link : https://goo.gl/forms/jeH573XGUSlktB6o1
​Thank you,
Debbie and Harry.
