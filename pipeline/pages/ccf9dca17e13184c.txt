We’re recruiting a Wellbeing Specialist who will provide mental health support to our companies
Nov 7
Written by Jan Baister
Crossroads Live has adopted a stringent anti-racism policy and is keen to ensure that our Global Majority members’ wellbeing is a key priority. We are committed to encouraging equality, diversity and inclusion among our employees, and eliminating unlawful discrimination.
That’s why we’re seeking a Wellbeing Specialist who ia a non-biased trained professional and will be responsible for providing mental health support through a confidential service to members of Crossroads Live’s theatrical companies, with a focus on our Global Majority company members.
For more info please visit our jobs page Closing date 10am 17th November.
United Kingdom
