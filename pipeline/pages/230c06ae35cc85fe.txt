Everybody say YEAH! Kinky Boots is returning to China!
International
Oct 16
Written by Firewatch
The Crossroads Live North America team were in Shanghai for the press launch of Kinky Boots which saw key press, and the infamous Duolingo Owl, take part in a live performance and Q&A with Omari Collins (Lola) and Noah Silverman (Charlie Price).
The Tony Award-winning musical, featuring score by Cyndi Lauper, book by Harvey Fierstein, and directed and choreographed by Jerry Mitchell, will strut its way into the magnificent Shanghai West Bund Grand Theatre from 3rd November – 15th November.
The tour will play at venues across China including Hefei, Nanjing, Beijing and Suzhou with more dates to be announced.
