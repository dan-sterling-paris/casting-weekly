A bold programme showcasing the future of dance
New approaches and propositions of what contemporary dance can be today, featuring 18 incredible artists performing works by internationally acclaimed artists Kor’sia, (La)Horde, and Matteo Marfoglia.
Verve turns 20! For two decades, Verve has launched dancers into the world, artists who now perform and collaborate with some of the most exciting companies across the globe.
This special show celebrates bold partnerships, past and present, with two visionary companies (La)Horde returning with People used to die, which was their first ever collaboration with a UK dance company; Kor’sia bringing The Garden back home after its international success; and, to top it off, a brand new creation by Matteo Marrfoglia completes the triple bill.
Presented by Northern School of Contemporary Dance.
