Grease The Musical to return for major new UK tour opening in Plymouth
Beloved musical classic launches 2027 tour at Theatre Royal Plymouth with Nikolai Foster directing
by
Staff Writer
April 30, 2026
Reading Time: 2 mins read
Grease Tour Image supplied by publcist
Grease The Musical will return to the road with a brand-new UK tour, opening at Theatre Royal Plymouth from Friday 23 April 2027.
The new tour is produced by Colin Ingram and will run through spring, summer and autumn 2027, with further dates still to be announced.
Following its Plymouth opening, the production will visit Cardiff, Newcastle, Wolverhampton, Stoke-on-Trent, Leeds, Belfast, Swansea, Southampton, Oxford, Liverpool, Bristol, Darlington, Glasgow, Edinburgh, Aberdeen, Sunderland, Nottingham, Birmingham, Norwich and Hull.
The production is directed by Curve’s Artistic Director Nikolai Foster, with choreography by Arlene Phillips, and book, music and lyrics by Jim Jacobs and Warren Casey.
Design is by Colin Richmond, with orchestrations and musical supervision by Sarah Travis, lighting design by Ben Cracknell, sound design by Tom Marshall and Richard Brooker, and casting by David Grindrod CDG.

Casting for the tour is yet to be announced.
The musical tells the story of leather-clad greaser Danny and girl-next-door Sandy, whose whirlwind summer romance is tested when they are unexpectedly reunited at Rydell High.
Featuring one of the most recognisable scores in musical theatre, the show includes hit songs such as Summer Nights, Greased Lightnin’, Hopelessly Devoted to You and You’re the One That I Want.
This new touring production is produced by Colin Ingram for InTheatre Productions and Curve.
