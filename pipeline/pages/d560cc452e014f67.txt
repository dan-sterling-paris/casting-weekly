Full Cast Announced for Legally Blonde The Musical UK and Ireland Tour
Amber Davies leads the Made at Curve production alongside Hannah Lowther, George Crawford, Karen Mavundukure and Adam Cooper.
by
Staff Writer
January 5, 2026
Reading Time: 2 mins read
The Cast of Legally Blonde image supplied by publicist
Producers ROYO and Curve have revealed the full cast for the brand-new Made at Curve production of Legally Blonde The Musical, which will tour the UK and Ireland from 7 February 2026 to 2 January 2027.
Amber Davies (Strictly Come Dancing; Pretty Woman) stars as Elle Woods, with musical theatre and TikTok sensation Hannah Lowther (SIX; Heathers: The Musical) playing Margot and Alternate Elle Woods.
George Crawford joins the cast as Emmett, Karen Mavundukure as Paulette and Adam Cooper as Callahan. Jamie Chatterton will play Warner, Annabelle Terry takes on Vivienne, and Jocasta Almgill appears as Brooke.
Other featured roles include Rosanna Harris as Serena, Remi Ferdinand as Pilar, Ty-Reece Stewart as Kyle and Bradley Delarosbel as Carlos. The ensemble features Keanna Bloomfield, James Lim, Aaron Shales, Jaime Tait, Daisy Twells, Louie Wood, Raiaz Fisher, Dylan Gordon-Jones, Lauren Hampton and Ollie Hart-Bradford.
Directed by Curve’s Artistic Director Nikolai Foster, the production will feature choreography by Leah Hill, musical supervision by Matt Spencer-Smith, set design by Colin Richmond, costume design by Tom Rogers, and wigs, hair and make-up by Sam Cox. Lighting design is by Ben Cracknell, sound design by Adam Fisher, and casting by Harry Blumenau CDG CDA.

The multi-award-winning musical features music and lyrics by Laurence O’Keefe and Nell Benjamin, with a book by Heather Hach, based on Amanda Browning’s novel and the iconic 2001 film starring Reese Witherspoon.
Legally Blonde The Musical follows Elle Woods on her journey from fashionista to Harvard Law student, proving that being true to yourself never goes out of style.
