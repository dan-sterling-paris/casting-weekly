Let your imagination take flight
Join us for the world premiere of a brand-new production of The Snowman™ as it lands in London for Christmas 2026.
For 32 years, The Snowman has enchanted theatre audiences, now, discover the next chapter of this much-loved story. Based on Raymond Briggs’ cherished book, The Rep’s sparkling new production features Howard Blake’s iconic score including the classic ‘Walking in the Air’, beautifully crafted puppets and of course, a gravity-defying friendship.
I want adults to become children and children never to forget it.Will Tuckett (Director)
When a young boy’s snowman magically comes to life, an unforgettable adventure begins. Together, they journey through the night, gliding hand-in-hand across the starlit sky. Along the way they encounter Father Christmas, a herd of friendly reindeer, and a joyful gathering of snow people beneath the shimmering Northern Lights, discovering a world of wonder, friendship and winter magic.
Directed and choreographed by Olivier Award-winning Will Tuckett ((West Side Story (RSC/The Sage Gateshead), Into the Woods (Royal Opera House), and The Nutcracker (National Ballet of Japan)), production design by the multi award-winning Anna Fleischle (Punch, 2:22 A Ghost Story, Everybody’s Talking About Jamie and Hangmen). With award-winning designer Tahra Zafar, best known for her recreation of Paddington Bear for the West End – bringing our iconic snowman to life.
Come and make a new snowman memory. Rediscover the joy of the story you love. Share the wonder of the magic you remember. And experience The Snowman with family and friends, as you’ve never seen it before.
For a strictly limited time only.

Relaxed Performances
A Relaxed Performance is a less formal theatre experience for anyone who would benefit from a more relaxed atmosphere to enjoy a show. They support noise and movement within the auditorium during a show, re-entry when needed so house lights are left on throughout, and a separate chill-out space for anyone who might need it at any time during their visit. We also provide a Visual Story so people can familiarise themselves with the theatre and key details about the show in advance.
