Six to embark on first ever outdoor tour
Divorced, beheaded, al fresco!
Alex Wood
|
Cardiff | Chester | London | Oxford
|
19 November 2026
The 2022 Queens at Hampton Court Palace, photo by Pamela Raith
Six the Musical will tour outdoor venues for the first time next summer, visiting ten UK locations from 4 June to 8 August 2027 as part of the show’s tenth anniversary celebrations.
The tour will feature a complete, fully staged production at castles, palaces, forests and seaside venues, finishing at Home Park, Hampton Court Palace. The show previously played a series of special concerts at Hampton Court Palace in 2022 (pictured above).
The company will be made up of performers drawn from Six productions around the world, accompanied by a 16-piece band. Casting is yet to be announced. The tour is produced in partnership with Cuffe and Taylor, the concert division of Live Nation.
Producer Kenny Wax said: “We are delighted to have partnered with Live Nation’s concert division Cuffe and Taylor on this exciting season of outdoor performances in ten truly stunning and individually unique venues to celebrate 10 extraordinary years of Six.”
Peter Taylor, co-founder of Cuffe and Taylor, said: “We’re thrilled to be taking Six on its first-ever outdoor tour. Pairing this phenomenal show with some of the UK’s most stunning historic and natural settings, plus a 16-piece band, makes for an unmissable summer night out, and there’s no better finale than a royal homecoming at Hampton Court Palace.”
Six is written by Toby Marlow and Lucy Moss and directed by Moss and Jamie Armitage. The show runs for 80 minutes and follows the six wives of Henry VIII as they tell their stories in the form of a pop concert.
Six at Hampton Court Palace, where the production will return next summer, photo by Pamela Raith
Choreography is by Carrie-Anne Ingrouille, with set design by Emma Bailey, costume design by Gabriella Slade, lighting design by Tim Deiling and sound design by Paul Gatehouse. Orchestrations are by Tom Curran, musical supervision is by Joe Beighton and casting is by Pearson Casting. The show is produced by Wax, Wendy and Andy Barnes and George Stiles.
The tour stops have been listed in our dedicated guide below, beginning in Scarborough in June, before heading to Delamere, Tetbury, Oxford, Lincoln, Milton Keynes, Margate, Halifax, Cardiff and London’s Hampton Court.
In London, Six is booking at the Vaudeville Theatre until November 2027 and also continues its UK and Ireland tour. On Broadway, it will close at the Lena Horne Theatre on 3 January 2027 after more than five years and over 2,100 performances. The show won Tony Awards for Best Original Score and Best Costume Design in 2022, and has won the WhatsOnStage Award for Best West End Show three times.
Tickets go on sale at 12 noon on Thursday 26 November 2026, with a 24-hour presale available from 12 noon on Wednesday 25 November.
