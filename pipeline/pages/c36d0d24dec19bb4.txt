Only Fools and Horses The Musical announces major UK and Ireland tour for 2027-28
Paul Whitehouse returns as Grandad on select dates as the hit musical embarks on a 40-plus venue tour across the UK and Ireland
by
Staff Writer
November 19, 2026
Reading Time: 2 mins read
Only Fools and Horses The Musical credit Johan Persson
The Trotters are heading back on the road as Only Fools and Horses The Musical returns for a major UK and Ireland tour from autumn 2027 through to summer 2028.
Following a sold-out four-year West End run and a successful regional tour, the musical will open in Bromley in September 2027 before visiting more than 40 cities and towns across the UK and Ireland.
Paul Whitehouse will reprise his role as Grandad on select dates, while Sam Lupton and Tom Major return as Del Boy and Rodney. Further casting is yet to be announced.
Directed by original West End director Caroline Jay Ranger, the musical brings audiences back to Peckham in 1989 as Del Boy searches for love, Rodney prepares to marry Cassandra and the Trotter family navigate a series of comic mishaps.
Based on John Sullivan’s beloved television sitcom, the stage adaptation features a script and original score by Jim Sullivan and Paul Whitehouse, alongside musical contributions from John Sullivan and Chas Hodges.

The musical played more than 1,000 performances at London’s Theatre Royal Haymarket and has become one of the UK’s most successful home-grown musical comedies.
Paul Whitehouse said: “We are delighted to announce that Only Fools and Horses The Musical is bounding back to the stage in 2027. From the West End to the regions, we have been overwhelmed by the love and support this brilliant show has received by generations of fans across the UK and Ireland, and we can’t wait to share more love and laughter on this tour. Don’t delay, get on the blower, and get a ticket for a truly cushty night out – only a 42 carat plonker would miss it!”
Featuring the famous theme tune, songs inspired by the world of the sitcom and many of its best-loved characters, Only Fools and Horses The Musical continues to delight fans old and new with its celebration of family, friendship and ambition.
