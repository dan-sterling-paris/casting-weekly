Full Cast Announced for 10th Anniversary UK Tour of Everybody’s Talking About Jamie
Kelle Bryan, Joanne Clifton and Divina De Campo join Ashcon Rahmani and Rebecca Trehearn for the anniversary tour of the hit musical
by
Staff Writer
September 23, 2026
Reading Time: 3 mins read
Everybody's Talking About Jamie Tour Cast Images supplied by publicist without credit
The full cast has been announced for the 10th Anniversary UK Tour of Everybody’s Talking About Jamie, which opens at Leeds Grand Theatre in January 2027, a decade after the musical first premiered at Sheffield Crucible.
Producer Nica Burns has revealed the company for the new tour, led by the previously announced Ashcon Rahmani as Jamie New and Rebecca Trehearn as his mum, Margaret.
Joining them are Kelle Bryan, who will play Miss Hedge from Leeds through to Southampton, and Joanne Clifton, who takes over the role from Cheltenham to Newcastle. Divina De Campo will star as Hugo/Loco Chanelle.
Also joining the company are Natasha Lewis as Ray, Mariam Pope as Pritti, Matthew Cavan as Tray Sophisticay, Daniel Jacob as Sandra Bollock, Jenson Steele as Laika Virgin, William Hazell as Dad, Kyle Dick as Dean, Ethan Casey-Clothier as Levi, Ellie Eames as Vicki, Uzuazo O’David as Cy, Grace Porter as Bex, Callum Saddler as Young Loco and first cover Jamie, Fin Scott as Mickey, Zaynab Shahid as Fatimah, Will Stevenson-Jones as Sayid, Zarina Thompson as Becca, Jáiden Lodge and Ynez Williams as swings, and Samantha Ivey as understudy Margaret, Miss Hedge and Ray.
Nica Burns said: “We are delighted to announce our wonderful new stars Kelle Bryan, Joanne Clifton and Divina di Campo who join the brilliant Ashcon Rahmani (Jamie) and Rebecca Trehearn (Mum). Everybody’s Talking About Jamie has been a huge success around the world, playing to audiences from all walks of life. A brilliant, uplifting musical for all.”

Kelle Bryan said: “I’m so delighted to take on this iconic musical role! Everybody’s Talking About Jamie is so relatable, relevant and heartfelt, but also fun and full of sass. I can’t wait to step into Miss Hedge’s fabulous red shoes and bring some ‘Edge to Hedge!’”
Joanne Clifton added: “I’m really looking forward to bringing Miss Hedge to life for our brilliant audiences! This role is a casting dream, and I can’t wait to join such a talented team of performers and makers for the show’s tenth anniversary! What an honour! Now CALM DOWN YEAR 11 and book your tickets QUICK!”
Divina De Campo said: “This show is so very special. Jamie’s amazing story – and the beautiful characters within – have impacted audiences world-over for a decade now. I’m excited to follow in the footsteps of some amazing past performers, and to bring Loco Chanelle into a new and glittering light.”
The anniversary production will also feature a brand-new song from composer Dan Gillespie Sells and writer Tom MacRae.
Since opening at Sheffield Crucible in 2017, Everybody’s Talking About Jamie has enjoyed a successful West End run, multiple UK tours, international productions and a feature film adaptation released by Amazon Studios.
Inspired by the true story of Jamie Campbell, the musical follows 16-year-old Jamie New, who dreams of becoming a drag queen despite facing prejudice and adversity. Supported by his loving mother and close friends, Jamie finds the confidence to step into the spotlight and embrace who he is.
The anniversary tour opens at Leeds Grand Theatre on 15 January 2027 before visiting Chester, Shrewsbury, Sunderland, Edinburgh, Wolverhampton, Wycombe, Sheffield, Manchester, Wimbledon, Liverpool, Truro, Eastbourne, Glasgow, Southampton, Cheltenham, Birmingham, Aylesbury, Cardiff, Nottingham, Leicester, Oxford, Woking, Bradford, Canterbury and Newcastle.
