Never judge a bird by its feathers…
This festive season, one brave little duckling embarks on an extraordinary mission to find a place where they truly belong.
Farm life has a very strict pecking order, and one duckling is too big, too dull, and too ugly to be accepted by the other animals. When the name-calling reaches the final straw, The Ugly Duckling marches out into the big wide world to see if the grass really is greener on the other side.
Battling through the seasons and facing their biggest adventure yet, our determined hero learns that being different is something to celebrate, and that the greatest journeys are the ones that lead us to ourselves.
This delightful adaptation by Katherine Chandler reimagines Hans Christian Andersen’s charming fairy tale and offers the perfect introduction to theatre for our younger audiences.
Caroline Wilkes, returns to direct our Door Christmas show following the huge success of Jack And The Magic Beanstalk and Little Red Riding Hood.

Creative Team
Adapted by
Katherine Chandler
Katherine Chandler
Katherine Chandler is an award-winning Welsh playwright and screenwriter. She has twice been a finalist for the Susan Smith Blackburn Prize, for Before it Rains and Parallel Lines, and has won the Theatre Critics of Wales Award, Writers’ Guild Playwright Award, Creative Wales Award and the Judges’ Award at the Bruntwood Prize for Playwriting for Bird, which was co-produced by Manchester Royal Exchange and Sherman Theatre.
Her plays include Lose Yourself, Peggy’s Song, produced as part of the NHS’s 70th anniversary celebrations, and her adaptation of The Silly Kings, which won Best Show for Young People at the Theatre Critics of Wales Awards.
Katherine was the inaugural winner of the BBC and National Theatre Wales Drama Award and has worked with companies including Clean Break, National Theatre Wales, Theatr Clwyd, Sherman Theatre and Bristol Old Vic.
Her radio drama The Invitation won Best Original Single Drama at the 2025 BBC Audio Drama Awards. She was also a creator and writer on King’s Pawn, the BBC Radio 4 comedy drama set in a Cardiff pawnbroker’s, which has been commissioned for a second series.
