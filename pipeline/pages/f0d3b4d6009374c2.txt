About A Christmas Carol - SECRET SEATS
What are Secret Seats?
Test your luck and get a bargain. With a secret seat you will find out on the day which seat you have. You may be in the Upper Circle, or you could end up in the Stalls!
Secret seats can go on sale at any time, but usually are available a month prior to the opening night.
When you book, you will receive a voucher to exchange at the theatre on the day of the performance.
Terms & Conditions
All seats will be sold as unreserved – like a lottery, you could be in any seat in the auditorium. You may get the best seats in the house, you may be in a restricted view seat.
Max 4 per booking (seats not guaranteed together)
Secret Seats tickets will go off sale at midnight the day before each performance
Non-refundable. Exchangeable only for an upgrade.
Chill winds sweep through the gas-lit streets of Victorian Edinburgh as Dickens’ beloved classic comes to life at The Lyceum for our 2026 festive season.
On Christmas Eve, three mysterious spirits whisk Ebenezer Scrooge away on a whirlwind journey through his past, present, and future. Will he discover the true meaning of Christmas before it’s too late?
Directed by our Award-winning Artistic Director, James Brining, let this festive classic fill you with cheer - join us for a Christmas tradition in the making.
A Royal Lyceum Theatre Edinburgh production
