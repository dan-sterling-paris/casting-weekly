My Neighbour Totoro Announces Full Cast For West End Transfer
We're excited to announce the full cast for the West End transfer of the hit show based on the Studio Ghibli classic.
Following two rapturously received, sell-out runs at the Barbican, the five-star, "awe-inspiring" (Time Out) My Neighbour Totoro opens at the Gillian Lynne Theatre from 8 March 2025, with the full cast now announced.
We're delighted to welcome Victoria Chen to the company to play the role of Mei. Returning to their roles are Ami Okumura Jones as Mei’s older sister Satsuki, Dai Tabuchi as Tatsuo, Jacqueline Tate as Granny, and Ai Ninomiya as the Singer.
