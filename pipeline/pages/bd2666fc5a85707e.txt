Punch confirms 2027 tour cast
They will star in the award-winning show!
Tanyel Gumushan
|
Tour
|
19 November 2026
Bailey West and Sally Ann Matthews, 1 – by Christopher Jeney, 2 – provided uncredited
Casting has been revealed for the 2027 tour of James Graham‘s Punch.
The production, directed by Adam Penford, premiered at Nottingham Playhouse in 2024 and later ran at the Young Vic. It subsequently transferred to the West End and Broadway in autumn 2025, before a UK tour.
Punch is based on the book Right from Wrong by Jacob Dunne and follows the events surrounding Dunne, a teenager from The Meadows in Nottingham, whose involvement in a violent incident in Old Market Square resulted in the death of James Hodgkinson. After serving a prison sentence, Dunne is approached by Hodgkinson’s parents, Joan and David, who ask to meet him, leading to a change in his life.
At the 2026 Olivier Awards, the show picked up the award for Best Play, with cast member Julie Hesmondhalgh winning Best Actress in a Supporting Role.
Set to lead the new tour is Nottingham actor Bailey West as Jacob, with Gail Kemp as Jacob’s Mum/Wendy/Sandra, Sally Ann Matthews as Joan, Mark Meadows as David and Mica Ricketts as Clare/Nicola, with Elan Butler reprising his role as Raf/Sam. They are joined by understudies Grace Elken and Laurence James-Davis.
Graham also received the award for Services to UK Theatre earlier this year at the WhatsOnStage Awards, you can watch a video below:
The original creative team returns, including production designer Anna Fleischle, lighting designer Robbie Butler, sound designer and composer Alexandra Faye Braithwaite, movement director Leanne Pinder and casting director Christopher Worrall.
The tour will open at Nottingham Playhouse from 25 January to 6 February, before visiting Bath Theatre Royal from 9 to 13 February, Birmingham Rep from 16 to 20 February, Chichester Festival Theatre from 23 to 27 February, Malvern Theatres from 2 to 6 March, Blackpool Grand Theatre from 9 to 13 March, and Curve, Leicester from 16 to 20 March.
After that, it will run at BEAM Hertford from 23 to 27 March, Citizens Theatre, Glasgow from 30 March to 3 April, the Gaiety Theatre, Dublin from 6 to 10 April, the Arts Theatre, Cambridge from 13 to 17 April, and the Belgrade Theatre, Coventry from 20 to 24 April.
