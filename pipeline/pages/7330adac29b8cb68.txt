Inspired by the 1985 classic The Breakfast Club comes a new dance theatre work by
Phoenix Dance Theatre.
Through a multicultural lens for a new generation, Sincerely, The Breakfast Club reveals how shared vulnerability can break down stereotypes and create moments of genuine connection. Set within the confines of a school library, five students emerge from their cliques, realising that they have more in common than they once thought.
As poetry seeps from the bookshelves and begins to shift the narrative, the characters are confronted and come to embrace resistance, insecurity, longing, and unexpected tenderness.
Following the success of the company’s award-nominated 2025 spring tour, Inside Giovanni’s Room, the creative team comes together again for this new work inspired by one of the most iconic coming-of-age stories, with music composed by Marc Strobel, dramaturgy from Tonderai Munyevu, alongside choreography by Marcus Jarrell Willis.
