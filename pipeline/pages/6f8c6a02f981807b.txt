Society of London Theatre & UK Theatre Conference 2026
UK
Jul 3
Written by Firewatch
The annual Society of London Theatre & UK Theatre Conference took place at London’s South Bank Centre this week and we were delighted to also attend the pre-conference drinks reception at the Royal Horseguards Hotel.
SOLT & UK Theatre Co-CEO Claire Walker
SOLT & UK Theatre Co-CEO Claire Walker welcomed fellow members, partners, and industry colleagues, setting the scene for the Conference with a rallying call to arms for this year’s theme: Ambitious Futures: Theatre in a Changing World.
Keynote Speech - Julie Hesmondhalgh
The day began with a rousing Keynote Speech from Olivier Award-winning actress and passionate campaigner Julie Hesmondhalgh on the importance of regional theatre and arts education for all.
SOLT & UK Theatre Co-CEO Claire Walker
Keynote Speech - Julie Hesmondhalgh
This year’s Conference programme examined the many challenges facing our industry in areas such as ensuring the ecology of nationwide touring can evolve and thrive, as well as the opportunities to grow and nurture future audiences and how we use AI as a sector.
Exporting Excellence panel - Despina Tsatsas, Georgia Gatti, Amit Lahav, Suzanne Walker, Ed McGovern
Our Group Business Development Director Ed McGovern took part in the panel on exporting excellence and how the UK can be used as a launchpad for globally ambitious productions, discussing the importance of collaboration internationally and how the UK is seen as world-leading in terms of talent, on, off and backstage.
As ever, it was inspiring to connect with colleagues across the industry, and we look forward to continuing these conversations throughout the year.
