RSC announces The Three Musketeers’ complete company
The festive caper is co-produced by Told By An Idiot
Alex Wood
|
Stratford-upon-Avon
|
13 October 2026
Laura Rogers and Simon Oskarsson, headshots supplied by the RSC
The Royal Shakespeare Company (RSC) and Told by an Idiot have announced the full cast for The Three Musketeers, adapted and directed by Told by an Idiot co-founder and artistic director Paul Hunter.
Spymonkey co-founder Aitor Basauri (as the Plot Teller), Simon Oskarsson (D’Artagnan) and Laura Rogers (Milady) join previously announced cast members Ayesha Antoine as Porthos, Patricia Rodríguez as Aramis and Sophie Russell as Athos. Basauri plays the Plot Teller, Oskarsson plays D’Artagnan and Rogers plays Milady.
The remaining cast comprises Kimmy Edwards as the Abbess and Landlady, Jasmine Elcock as the Queen, Michael Elcock as Rochefort, Matt Gavan as the King, Nick Haverson as Cardinal Richelieu and Jean Claude, Martin Hyder as Monsieur Treville and Landlord, Natalie Kimmerling as Kitty, David Olaniregun as Buckingham and Tanvi Virmani as Constance. Greg Bernstein, Kelise Gordon-Harrison and Theo Reece complete the company as ensemble members.
Based on Alexandre Dumas’ novel, the production will run at the Royal Shakespeare Theatre in Stratford-upon-Avon from 28 November 2026 to 9 January 2027, with a press night on 8 December.
The production is adapted and directed by Hunter, with a creative team comprising set and costume designer Michael Vale, lighting designer Aideen Malone, composer Ian Ross, sound designer Tingying Dong, choreographer Jennifer Jackson, physical comedy consultant Jos Houben, dramaturg Stewart Pringle and casting director Matthew Dewsbury CDG.
