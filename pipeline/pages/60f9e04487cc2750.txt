First cast revealed for the reimagined one-part production of Harry Potter and the Cursed Child in London
Published on Sep 20th 2026
AsAs the historic, record-breaking two-part production ends its ground-breaking ten-year run, it’s time to usher in a new era for the Cursed Child in London’s West End.
It’s certainly a momentous week for Harry Potter and the Cursed Child. Not only did the final performance of the award-winning original two-part production take place over the weekend, but we can also reveal the new cast stepping on to the Palace Theatre stage from 9 October 2026 in the reimagined one-part production. Before we celebrate a triumphant ending, let’s talk about a new beginning.
Introducing the Year 10 company
The reimagined one-part production will tell the whole adventurous story in one complete performance, promising to be a spectacular, unforgettable experience. But who will be the first actors to bring this production to life? Permit us to unfurl our scroll, Professor McGonagall style, as we welcome these stars to a new year at Hogwarts…
New cast members:
Ethan Rouse as Albus Potter
Zoë Rainey as Ginny Potter
Hasia Akwaboah as Rose Granger-Weasley
Adam Jackson-Smith as Draco Malfoy
Bill Stanley as Scorpius Malfoy
Continuing cast members:
David Ricardo-Pearce as Harry Potter
Thomas Aldridge as Ron Weasley
Naana Agyei Ampadu as Hermione Granger
They will be joined by: Dewayne Jameson Adams, David Annen, Jacqueline Beaumont, Jensen Bedford, Angeline Bell, Tatum Confrey, Robert Curtis, Nick Dennington, Shakhai Estwick, Miah Hasselbaink, Tim Hibberd, Becky Hoyle, Max Hutchinson, Danny Ivy, Emma Louise Jones, Laura June Ness, Louise Ludgate, Shreya Lallu, Nathan Muwowo, Jocelyn Prah, Claire Redcliffe, Ewan Rutherford, Joshua Talbot, Nathaniel Tan, Callum Tempest, Alex Tomkins, Jake Tuesley and Aidan Garrett Wilkins.
The one-part production is currently booking to 27 June 2027; for more information and to be one of the first to get your tickets, head to the Harry Potter and the Cursed Child box office.
A final bow after 10 years
As we welcome a magical new era for the Cursed Child, it’s only fitting to celebrate the phenomenal achievement of the original two-part production’s ten-year run. Over the last decade Harry Potter and the Cursed Child has become a global success having been by seen more than 14 million people worldwide, including over 2.5 million at its London home. The multi award-winning two-part production had its final sold-out performance on Sunday 20 September and the audience reception at the curtain-call was deservingly jubilant.
Well, that was emotional… Anyone else feel like they got some Floo powder in their eyes? Congratulations to every single person who brought this historic, record-breaking production to life over the last 10 years. Now on to the next adventure.
