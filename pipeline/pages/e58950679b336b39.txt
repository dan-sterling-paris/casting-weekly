Cautions: Nina: By Whatever Means contains flash photography and haze, as well as scenes of domestic violence and racism.
Cassa Pancho’s Ballet Black returns with a thrilling triple bill featuring the revival of Mthuthuzeli November’s acclaimed Nina: By Whatever Means, celebrating the extraordinary life, music and spirit of Nina Simone.
Isabela Coracy returns to her Olivier Award-winning role as Nina, a performance that also earned her a Black British Theatre Award, alongside the Ballet’s own award for the production.
Completing the programme are two contrasting Ballet Black favourites: Martin Lawrance’s intense and intricately charged Captured, set to Shostakovich, and Sophie Laplane’s playful, stylish and irresistibly energetic CLICK!
Three distinctive ballets showcasing the range, individuality and unmistakable spirit of Ballet Black.
