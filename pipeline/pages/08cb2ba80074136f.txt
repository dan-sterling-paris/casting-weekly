One month until our first pantomime of the season, Beauty and the Beast, begins at Kings Theatre Glasgow
Oct 26
Written by Jan Baister
As quintessentially part of the UK festivities as Brussel sprouts for Christmas lunch, this year Crossroads Pantomimes presents 24 incredible productions across the United Kingdom, featuring a glittering array of star names, renowned creative teams, talented casts and musicians, and passionate production teams as well over 30,000 pyrotechnic items and 15,000 costumes!
Crossroads Pantomimes remains the world’s biggest pantomime producer and with tickets sales on track, it’s clear that our audiences appetite for riotous family entertainment remains as keen as ever!
For more info and the latest news check out pantomime.com
