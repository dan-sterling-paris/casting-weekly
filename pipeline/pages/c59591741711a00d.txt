Annie the Musical takes part in the first Macao International Children’s Arts Festival
Jul 31
Written by Firewatch
The cast of Annie plus Kevin (Sandy the dog) were excited to present the production in Macao at a special launch where they performed Tomorrow and It’s the Hard Knock Life for members of the press and Acting President of IC, Cheang Kai Meng.
The musical is produced by Crossroads Live North America and Broadway Asia, under the patronage of the Secretariat for Social Affairs and Culture of the Government of the Macao SAR, organised by the Cultural Affairs Bureau (IC, from the Portuguese acronym) and will perform 12 performances from 2 – 14 August at the Macao Cultural Centre Grand Auditorium as part of the 1st Macao International Children’s Arts Festival.
The festival is committed to building Macao as a city of performing arts, celebrating art and creativity, and we were delighted to be part of it.
