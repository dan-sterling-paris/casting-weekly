FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
When is the show booking until?
TICKET FAQS
Burlesque The Musical at The Arts at Marble Arch is booking until 31st January 2027.
Burlesque The Musical UK Tour is running until May 2027. See full tour dates here
When will I receive my tickets?
Patience is a virtue, but we know you’re itching for that spotlight! Your tickets are handled by our fabulous individual ticket agent. Since every box office has its own rhythm, please check with your ticket agent.
Can I exchange or refund my tickets?
The show must go on, but we understand if your plans change! Whether you can swap your seats or get a refund depends entirely on the terms and conditions of your specific ticket vendor. Check their website to see what’s possible—they hold the keys!
Are Group Bookings available?
Absolutely! If you're looking to bring the whole gang to witness the glitz and glamour, here is how to secure your spot in the spotlight:
For the London run: Our team at The Arts at Marble Arch is waiting in the wings to assist you! Simply give them a ring at 020 7836 8463 or email boxoffice@artstheatrewestend.co.uk. They’ll make sure your group is seated in style.
‍
On the Tour: Every city has its own secrets! For group rates and availability while we’re on the road, please check the specific venue website for your chosen tour stop. Each theatre handles their own guest list, so head straight to the source!
Can I use theatre tokens to pay for my tickets?
You certainly can! The Arts Theatre is delighted to accept SOLT Theatre Tokens. Whether you’ve been gifted them by an admirer or saved them for a rainy day, it’s time to cash in.
On tour, please check the venue website.
What happens if my show is cancelled?
In the unlikely event that we have to cancel, don’t you worry—we won't leave you in the dark!
The box office will endeavour to inform ticket holders as far in advance as possible. They’ll be in touch to help you exchange your tickets or to arrange a refund so you can join us another time. If you secured your spot through a third-party agent, they will be in touch. While we hate to disappoint, please note that we cannot be held liable for any extra costs like travel or accommodation.
I have a question about my ticket – who do I contact?
Don't be shy! If your query is about a ticket purchased, you’ll need to contact the specific box office or ticket agent where you made your purchase.
Does Burlesque The Musical offer concessionary tickets?
We believe everyone deserves a bit of sparkle! However, the availability and types of concessionary tickets vary depending on where you're seeing the show. Always check with the specific venue (whether it's our new London home at Marble Arch or a stop on our fabulous UK tour).
And don’t forget, many venues offer special rates for students, seniors, or theatre-loving youngsters, so it’s always worth a look at their booking page or a quick call to their box office team!
Is there a dress code?
While there’s no official dress code, we love it when our audiences "show a little more!" Whether you want to rock sequins and feathers or keep it smart-casual, you’re welcome here.
SHOW FAQS
Will the cast be the same for the tour and the London production?
Each production features its own world-class cast of West End and international performers. While the "family" is different, the talent is equally electrifying!
What is the running time of the show?
The show runs for approximately 2 hours and 30 minutes, including one 20-minute interval.
Is the show suitable for children?
Burlesque is recommended for ages 12+. The production contains some mature themes, "sizzling" choreography, and language that reflects the grit and glamour of the club scene.
Does the show have any special effects?
Yes! Including intense strobing, smoke effects, and a "stage-high" video design.

Will I able to purchase merchandise at the show?
Yes, indeed! You can absolutely take a piece of the magic home with you. We’ll have a glittering array of official merchandise available at the theatre, so you can strut out with more than just the songs in your head.
We’ll have even more exciting news about our collection very soon. Be sure to keep checking back for updates on exactly what treasures will be waiting for you.
ACCESSIBLITY FAQS
Does Burlesque offer accessible shows?
Absolutely! We believe the show is for everyone. We have a full lineup of Audio Described, BSL Interpreted, Captioned, and Relaxed performances scheduled for both our London run at The Arts at Marble Arch and on tour. Check with the venue direct for their schedule of access assisted performances.
Is there accessible seating or wheelchair spaces?
Yes! Booking is handled by each individual venue's box office so get in touch directly with the venue of your choice to find out full details and availability.
Can my Essential Companion join the party?
Of course! Most of our venues offer a complimentary or discounted ticket for Essential Companions. Please check with the individual venues box office.
Is the show "extra" when it comes to lights and sound?
It wouldn’t be Burlesque without a bit of drama! Expect strobe lighting, flashes, theatrical haze, and some seriously powerhouse sound levels. If you have specific sensitivities, please chat with the individual venue box office teams.
Will I be able to hear every note?
Most venues are equipped with the assisted listening technology. Please check with the individual venue.
