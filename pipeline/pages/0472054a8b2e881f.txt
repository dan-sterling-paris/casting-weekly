Crossroads Live touches down in China
International
Jun 1
Written by Firewatch
Executive Producer Alex Halpern is zig-zagging all over China this month as the country re-ignites conversations surrounding future international tours and partnerships in earnest.
Pivotal to the trip was meeting with Poly Culture and Poly Theatre Management, who operate more than 80 theatres in 65 cities across the country, and who outlined their long-term goals surrounding further exciting growth of the entertainment industry in China.
