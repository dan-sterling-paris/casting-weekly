Biography
Catriona Dickie CDG cast the hit feature, CATHERINE CALLED BIRDY, for Amazon Prime – which garnered rave reviews. For her work on the film, Catriona won the “Outstanding Achievement in Casting – Studio or Independent Feature – Comedy” award at the prestigious Casting Society of America (ARTIOS) Awards.
Her current slate includes BLACK AGNES (from the producers of DEN OF THIEVES, POWER, and MARGIN CALL), CHAINBREAKER (MPH Productions), and AN AUSTRIAN HOLIDAY, for which she’s attached two A list stars.
Upcoming projects also include GROUNDED, a feature set to film in Canada, and indie comedy feature BORN TO BINGO.
Recent work spans short films such as MARRIAGE UNPLUGGED (Jury Prize winner at Leeds Film Festival), CLIFF EDGE, and UN-BECOMING, alongside features like THE HUSBAND SWAP and THE PERFECT ESCAPE.
She cut her teeth as a Casting Associate for Nina Gold and Robert Sterne – working on projects such as the gripping BBC One series, THE RESPONDER, and the tense legal drama feature – THE MAURITANIAN. Before that, she worked as a Casting Associate for Rachel Freck, casting the beloved Apple TV+ series, TRYING, among other prestige comedy series.
In addition to her casting work, Catriona has helped mentor under-represented young people in the UK who have an interest in the Film and TV industry via the award winning charity ‘Arts Emergency’.
