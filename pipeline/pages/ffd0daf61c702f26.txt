Hamilton announces casting for Korean Premiere
International
Oct 30
Written by Firewatch
The producers are delighted to announce casting for the Korean Premiere of HAMILTON which opens on 17 December 2026, running through until 16 January 2027 at Dream Theatre in Busan. The production then moves to the Grand Theater at Sejong Center in Seoul from 21 January until 20 March 2027.
Hamilton alumni from Broadway, the West End, Australia, and the US and UK touring companies make up this illustrious international cast, alongside several newcomers.
Jason Arrow reprises his role as Alexander Hamilton, having spent four years portraying the character with both the Original Australian and International companies. Arrow first took on the role for the show's Australian premiere in 2021. Joining him is Jay Perry as Aaron Burr, Hamilton’s narrator and rival. Perry, who previously played the role in the West End production, won Best Male Actor at the Black British Theatre Awards for his performance. Nicola Espallardo also returns as Angelica Schuyler, following her portrayal in the West End production, and after having previously garnered acclaim for her portrayal of Elphaba in the West End production of Wicked.
The principal lineup includes Mark Anderson (King George), Jordan Castle (George Washington), John Devereaux (Hercules Mulligan/James Madison), Nicola Espallardo (Angelica Schuyler), Jake Halsey-Jones (John Laurens/Philip Hamilton), Jorrel Javier (Marquis de Lafayette/Thomas Jefferson), Jay Perry (Aaron Burr), Jen Sese (Eliza Hamilton), and Charlotte Mary Wen (Peggy Schuyler/Maria Reynolds).
Completing the Company are Richard Appiah-Sarpong, Jazmin Bird, Leslie Garcia Bowman, Conroe Brooks, Kyerron Dixon-Bassey, Jeffery Duffy, Michael Samarie George, Richard Logun, Ella McLaughlin, Jazz Miller, Chris Otim, Castell Parker, Bradley Perret, Izzy Read, Joshua Robinson, Samantha Shuma, Lucy-Anne Stacey, Sylvie Stenson, Garrett Tennant, Raven Thomas, and Jack Whitehead.
HAMILTON features book, music, and lyrics by Lin-Manuel Miranda, direction by Thomas Kail, choreography by Andy Blankenbuehler, and musical supervision and orchestrations by Alex Lacamoire. In addition to its 11 Tony Awards®, it has won Grammy® and Olivier Awards, and the Pulitzer Prize for Drama.
HAMILTON features scenic design by David Korins, costume design by Paul Tazewell, lighting design by Howell Binkley, sound design by Nevin Steinberg, hair and wig design by Charles G. LaPointe, and casting by Telsey + Company, Bethany Knox, CSA and Lucy Casson CDG.
The musical is produced by Jeffrey Seller, Sander Jacobs, Jill Furman and The Public Theater. The Korean engagement is co-produced by S&Co and presented by Clipservice with general management by Crossroads Live.
