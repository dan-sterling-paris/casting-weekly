Dead Special - Beetlejuice opens in the West End!
UK
Jun 2
Written by Firewatch
We had the time of our afterlife at the Gala of beloved spectacular Broadway musical Beetlejuice, which has now opened in the West End.
Fans and audience members were greeted outside the Prince Edward Theatre by a flash mob of mourners and a hearse, complete with floral name tribute to our favourite irreverent ghost with the most, Beetlejuice.
Following a riotous reception and standing ovation guests decamped to shake, shake, shake, Senora at The Crypt at St Martin’s in the Fields for a suitably spooky end to the evening.

He's dead trouble, but if you really want to feel alive, just say his name three times…
