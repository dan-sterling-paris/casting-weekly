Just In Time nominated for 6 Tony Awards
North America
May 27
Written by Firewatch
Following opening to sensational reviews at Circle in the Square, Just In Time has been nominated for 6 TONY AWARDS!
Jonathan Groff is sensational as Bobby Darin. Photo credit: Matthew Murphy and Evan Zimmerman
Gracie Lawrence stuns as Connie Francis. Photo credit: Matthew Murphy and Evan Zimmerman
The TONY AWARD nominations include:
Best Lead Actor in a Musical - Jonathan Groff
Best Featured Actress in a Musical - Gracie Lawrence
Best Orchestrations - Andrew Resnick and Michael Thurber
Best Scenic Design of a Musical - Derek McLane
Best Costume Design of a Musical - Catherine Zuber
Best Sound Design of a Musical - Peter Hylenski
Developed and directed by Tony Award winner Alex Timbers (Moulin Rouge, Beetlejuice), Just In Time is an "utterly electrifying” (Entertainment Weekly) new musical that immerses audiences into an intimate nightclub "so seductive in ambiance that you find yourself not wanting to go back outside" (Chicago Tribune). Complete with iconic Bobby Darin hits including “Beyond the Sea,” “Mack the Knife,” “Splish Splash,” and “Dream Lover" Just In Time is "a helluva good time at the theater" (Time Out).
Wishing everyone the best of luck on 9 June!
