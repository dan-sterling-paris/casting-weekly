Synopsis
Share:
“The trouble with Christmas is it’s too exciting!”
Daisy LOVES Christmas! She can’t wait for all the festive fun to begin, and this year is extra special because she’s in the school Christmas play with SEVEN whole words to learn.
But when Daisy and her best friend Gabby decide the Baby Jesus doll isn’t quite right, they come up with a plan that can only lead to trouble.
Based on Kes Gray’s much-loved DAISY AND THE TROUBLE WITH CHRISTMAS, this brilliantly funny adventure is full of Christmas chaos for children and their grown-ups.
Whether it’s their first theatre trip, a special family outing or a treat with grandparents, this mischief-filled Made at Curve production promises big laughs, brilliant storytelling and plenty of Daisy-sized fun.
Bursting with energy and packed with seasonal silliness, it’s the perfect way to celebrate the most wonderful time of the year.
Age guidance: 3+
Running time: 1 hour (with no interval)
