Celebrating 2023 with #TheStage100
Jan 4
Written by Firewatch
We’re delighted to begin 2024 with entries for both David Ian and Michael Harrison in #TheStage100, which celebrates the most influential figures working in UK theatre and recognises their achievements throughout 2023.
With new West End productions of Mean Girls and Hello, Dolly, a brand-new tour of Chitty Chitty Bang Bang, continuing tours of The Drifters Girl and Jesus Christ Superstar, plus 24 pantomimes in the UK, productions of Wicked, Grease, The Rocky Horror Show, Chicago, and The Odd Couple in Australia, Love Never Dies in China and Pretty Woman, Annie, The Grinch and many more in North America, 2024 is shaping up to be a very exciting year for ‘one of the largest producers of commercial theatre worldwide!’
