At DOB Casting we are heavily committed to meeting as many performers as possible. Unfortunately, we are always bound to a certain number of days where we can audition per project.
Over the next month, we will be announcing a day of meetings dedicated to those who we haven’t met before.
We will aim to meet as many performers as we can with the view to getting to know you better and have more of an understanding of who is out there. Whether it be for musicals, plays, singers, dancers or circus acts, we want to meet you. After all, we can’t do our job without you!
To register your interest, please go to the following link and we will be in touch! We may not be able to meet everyone who responds, but we will try to see as many people as possible in each session.
https://goo.gl/forms/93tU4HmH2w98jOSH3
With all best wishes,
Debbie, Harry, Claire and Nicky.
