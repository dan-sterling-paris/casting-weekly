Enjoy Spectacular Five-Star Festive Fun this Holiday Season!
Pantomimes
Dec 19
Written by Firewatch
Crossroads Pantomimes now have an incredible 24 all-star festive productions live across the UK this December and January!
There’s a classic story for everyone, from Cinderella, to Snow White and the Seven Dwarves, Sleeping Beauty, Peter Pan, Dick Whittington, Beauty and the Beast, Goldilocks and the Three Bears and Jack and the Beanstalk, all served with a unique panto twist. For many audience members this can be their first experience of the wonder of theatre, which is why it has to be extra-special, and with 120,000 glittering costumes, breath-taking sets, and a host of star leads, it truly is.
Like Santa’s elves it’s always Christmas for the Crossroads Pantomimes team who are busy putting next years fabulous family entertainment on sale now. Check out pantomime.com for more information.
Happy Holidays everyone!
