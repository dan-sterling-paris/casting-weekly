I SAY! THERE'S BEEN A MURDER!
The Famous Five grew up and grew apart.
When Dick, now a hardened PI, gets in over his head, he turns to his sister Anne for help. Do they have what it takes to solve the crime? A Film Noir for the stage, full of gangsters, intrigue, and lashings of tinned pineapple. With additional voices by Alistair McGowan. Edinburgh Fringe sell-out show. Nominated for the Funny Women Awards. Supported by Barnsley Civic.
Rachel E. Thorn writes for BBC Radio (Dead Ringers, The Now Show). She’s been nominated for the Kenneth Branagh Award, the Female Pilot Club, the Kay Mellor Fellowship and the BBC’s Alfred Bradley Award.
