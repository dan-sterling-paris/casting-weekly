SIX The Musical announces first-ever outdoor tour to celebrate tenth anniversary
The global hit musical will visit castles, palaces, forests and open-air venues across the UK in summer 2027
by
Staff Writer
November 19, 2026
Reading Time: 2 mins read
Six Outdoor Image supplied by publicist without credit
SIX The Musical will make history next summer with its first-ever outdoor tour, as part of celebrations marking the show’s tenth anniversary.
Producers have announced SIX Outdoor – The Royal Homecoming Tour, which will bring the complete fully staged production to ten outdoor venues across the UK between June and August 2027.
The tour will visit a range of locations including Scarborough Open Air Theatre, Delamere Forest, Westonbirt Arboretum, Blenheim Palace, Lincoln Castle, Cardiff Castle and The Piece Hall, before culminating at Home Park, Hampton Court Palace.
Featuring a specially assembled cast drawn from SIX productions around the world, the outdoor staging will also include a 16-piece live band, offering audiences a new way to experience the musical’s internationally celebrated pop score.
The award-winning show reimagines the six wives of Henry VIII as modern pop stars, transforming centuries of royal history into an energetic 80-minute concert-style musical.

Kenny Wax said: “We are delighted to have partnered with Live Nation’s concert division Cuffe and Taylor on this exciting season of outdoor performances in ten truly stunning and individually unique venues to celebrate 10 extraordinary years of SIX”
Peter Taylor, Co-Founder of Cuffe & Taylor, said: “We’re thrilled to be taking SIX on its first-ever outdoor tour. Pairing this phenomenal show with some of the UK’s most stunning historic and natural settings, plus a 16-piece band, makes for an unmissable summer night out, and there’s no better finale than a royal homecoming at Hampton Court Palace.”
Tickets for SIX Outdoor go on sale at midday on 26 November 2026, with an exclusive presale available to fans who sign up through the production website from 25 November.
The announcement comes as SIX continues its global success, with productions running across the UK, Europe, Asia and Australia, alongside billions of streams of its cast recordings worldwide.
