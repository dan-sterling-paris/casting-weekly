what to expect.
A brand-new haunting play, GHOST HOUSE follows three best friends who, after months of travelling the world, arrive at a remote island lodge for their very last stop. It should be a celebration. Instead, it becomes something far more sinister.
When an unseen presence begins to make itself known, they realise they are not alone. As fear tightens its grip, loyalties fracture and buried secrets rise to the surface. Survival instincts take over, and the question shifts from what’s out there...
to who can you trust in here? GHOST HOUSE is a chilling blend of supernatural horror and psychological thriller-where the greatest threat may not be the ghost at all.
