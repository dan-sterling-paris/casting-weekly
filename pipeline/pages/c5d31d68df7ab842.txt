Content Advisories
This show contains themes of grief and loss, missing persons, and one scene of discovering human remains. May contain loud noises and flashing lights.
Alma is a mother searching for her missing daughter, one of the many people who have disappeared without trace in Mexico. Caught between absence and hope, her days become a relentless search for answers. But Alma refuses to give up.
As she looks for her daughter, Alma finds others living through the same nightmare. Families come together, strangers become lifelines and what begins as a solitary struggle grows into a collective act of resistance.
Created by internationally acclaimed, multi-award-winning theatre companies Ad Infinitum (UK) and El Ingenio del Caldero (Mexico), Until I Find You combines fierce, raw physicality with powerful storytelling.
Following its world premiere in Mexico, the production has received multiple awards and nominations, including Best Show, Best One-Person Show, Best Movement, Play of the Year and Best Direction.
Until I Find You is co-produced by Ad Infinitum, El Ingenio del Caldero, and Teatro UNAM. This tour is made possible with support from Arts Council England’s National Lottery Fund.
