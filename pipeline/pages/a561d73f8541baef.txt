★ ★ ★ ★ ★ “This is a rockin', rollin' roller coaster. Fasten your festive seat belts” Wirral Globe
BEAUTY, A BEAST & A WHOLE LOT OF BEATS!
Get ready for a riot of riffs, rhythm, and romance as Beauty and the Beast roars onto stage as the legendary Everyman Rock ’n’ Roll panto for 2026.
Our outrageously talented actor-musicians bring you a show that’s part rock concert, part pop spectacular, and all-out family fun. Made just for Liverpool, this is the fairytale you know and love… but turned all the way up to eleven!
A little naughty, a lot loud, and totally live, you can expect powerhouse vocals, electrifying tunes and some wild costumes. So be our guest, grab your crew and head to Hope Street, for a monstrously good time.

Joshie Harriette
Lighting Designer
Joshie Harriette is an award-winning British lighting designer, and choreographic light artist, whose innovative work is redefining the relationship between movement, performance, and light. Renowned for his distinctive approach to “choreographing with light,” he creates visually arresting and emotionally intelligent designs that place illumination at the heart of the storytelling process.
Working across theatre, dance, opera, live performance, and immersive experiences, Joshie has established himself as one of the UK’s most exciting contemporary design voices. His work has been seen at many of the country’s leading cultural institutions, including the Royal Shakespeare Company, Royal Ballet, Royal Court, Factory International, Queen Elizabeth Hall, National Theatre, Sadler’s Wells, Lyric Hammersmith and in the West End.
Liverpool Everyman & Playhouse credits include: Shirley Valentine (Everyman).
Notable productions include: Cyrano de Bergerac (West End), The New Real (Royal Shakespeare Company), Dracula (Lyric Hammersmith), Burlesque The Musical (UK Tour), Cake: The Marie Antoinette Playlist (West End), Legends of Them (Royal Court), Sucker Punch (National Theatre UK Tour), Nutcracker (Southbank Centre).
Dance credits include: Pigeons at Sadler’s Wells East, Dream Ballets at Regent’s Park Open Air Theatre,The Brown Suite for Fundamentally Dance, Proper Conduct for English National Ballet, International DraftWorks for Royal Ballet & Opera where his choreographic approach to light has been showcased alongside emerging dance-makers and contemporary ballet artists.
His achievements have been recognised with the Black British Theatre Award for Best Lighting Design (2023), the Profile Award for Outstanding Achievement in Dance (2025) for Pigeons, and selection as a Factory International Fellow (2023).
Through a singular artistic vision, Joshie continues to push the boundaries of contemporary performance, establishing light as an expressive and performative force in its own right.
Joshie was the winner of the ‘Lighting Recognition Award’ at Black British Theatre Awards 2023 and was a 2023 fellow with Factory International.

Yvonne Lawlor
Casting Director
Yvonne is a Casting Director working across TV, Film, Theatre and Commercials. With 10 years in the industry, she’s worked with some of the best whilst carving her own career.
As a proud Brummy, and with offices in both London and Manchester – she is passionate about bridging the gap between the North, the South and the Midlands, making sure actors from every corner of the UK have the same platform and opportunities.
As part of the LGBTQIA+ community, Yvonne understands the importance of diversity and inclusion, and this sits at the heart of her work: everyone deserves equal opportunity regardless of race, sex, sexuality, gender, class or disability.
With a sharp eye for talent and a love for developing fresh voices, Yvonne thrives on script and the magic of storytelling. She wants audiences to feel seen on screen, to recognise themselves and their worlds on the telly.
Some of her proudest Casting credits include: House Of David (Amazon); Malpractice S2 (ITV); Picture This (Amazon); Shardlake (Disney); The Doll Factory (Paramount); I May Destroy You (BBC/HBO) and Small Prophets (BBC).

Juliette Collins
YEP Trainee Assistant Director
Juliette is an actor/writer and Screen Acting graduate of LIPA. She loves making comedy, sketches and anything a little bit strange. Her work has featured at the Edinburgh Fringe, national film festivals, and in physical theatre projects with Tmesis and Ugly Bucket. She co-created and stars as a nun in the online mockumentary The Nun Files. She is particularly interested in character, the unexpected and all things wonderfully weird.
She is thrilled to be joining the Everyman as a YEP Trainee Assistant Director on the panto!
