Christmas Comes Early at the Crossroads Pantomimes Summer Party
Pantomimes
Jun 12
Written by Firewatch
David Ian, CEO Crossroads Live Group and Crossroads Pantomimes CEO Michael Harrison
Michael Harrison and the team at Crossroads Pantomimes celebrated the season early with a drinks party at the Grand Saloon at Theatre Royal Drury Lane.
Cast alumni joined teams from each of the twenty-one venues that play host to the productions each year, together with staff from Crossroads Pantomimes and other friends and colleagues.
CEO of Crossroads Live Group David Ian paid tribute to Michael Harrison’s continued leadership of Crossroads Pantomimes stating.
“As we look ahead to another stellar season of pantomimes there is no one I’d rather work alongside than Michael, his vast knowledge, enthusiasm and experience means he embodies the very spirit of panto.”
CEO of Crossroads Pantomimes Michael Harrison raised a toast to everyone.
“We so rarely all get the chance to gather together so as we gear up for another busy pantomime season I wanted to personally thank everyone that makes the magic happen each year, without you none of this is possible.”
