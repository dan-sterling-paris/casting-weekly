Biography
Stuart’s work as a Casting Director can currently be seen on Much Ado About Nothing (Broadway), One Day (Garrick Theatre), Into The Woods (Noël Coward Theatre), Oh, Mary! (Trafalgar Theatre), Cabaret (Kit Kat Club/Playhouse Theatre), Paranormal Activity (Ambassadors Theatre) and the UK Tour of Mrs Doubtfire.
Stuart has won five CDG Awards: in 2025 for Best Casting in Musical Theatre for Fiddler on the Roof, in 2024 for Best Casting in Regional Theatre for Standing at the Sky’s Edge, in 2023 for Best Casting in Musical Theatre for Cabaret and Best Casting in Regional Theatre for East Is East and in 2021 for Best Casting in Theatre for Cyrano de Bergerac. He has been included in The Stage 100 list of the most influential people in British theatre three times and Into the Woods, which he cast at the Bridge Theatre, won the first Critics’ Circle Award for Best Ensemble.
