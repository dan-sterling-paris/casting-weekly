About the show
One Day The Musical is a new musical adaptation of David Nicholls’ bestselling novel, playing a strictly limited run at the Garrick Theatre, starring Jamie Muscato as Dexter Mayhew and Sharon Rose as Emma Morley.
Based on Nicholls’ 2009 novel and the Focus Features film, the musical follows Emma and Dexter after they meet on their graduation night on 15 July 1988. Every year on the same date, audiences catch up with the pair as their friendship evolves through love, heartbreak, ambition and life’s unexpected twists. Spanning two decades, the story explores missed chances, personal growth and the enduring connection between two people whose lives remain intertwined.
With a book by David Greig, direction by Max Webster and 21 brand-new songs by Amanda and Abner Ramirez of Johnnyswim, this is a heartfelt new musical about the days that change everything.
Why it’s worth the watch
A beloved love story reborn as a musical, tracing two lives across twenty years and one date.

Frequently asked questions
Is it based on the book and film?
Yes. It is a new musical adaptation of David Nicholls' bestselling 2009 novel, which was also made into a film and a television series. It brings the much-loved story of Emma and Dexter to the stage for the first time as a musical.
Do I need to have read the book or seen the adaptations?
No. The show tells the story clearly on its own, so newcomers are welcome, while fans of the book will know the emotional turns that lie ahead. Either way, it stands complete in itself.
How does the one-day structure work?
The story revisits Emma and Dexter on the same date, 15 July, once a year over two decades, catching up with their friendship, love and missed chances each time. That single recurring day is what gives the story its distinctive, poignant shape.
Is it a sad show?
It is a bittersweet romance rather than a straightforwardly happy one, and it is known for its emotional gut-punch, so a few tissues would not go amiss. It is also warm, funny and full of life along the way.
Where are the best seats at the Garrick Theatre?
The Garrick is an intimate, handsome house well suited to this close, character-driven story, and the stalls and dress circle both give a fine view. You can compare every price band when you pick your seats above.
